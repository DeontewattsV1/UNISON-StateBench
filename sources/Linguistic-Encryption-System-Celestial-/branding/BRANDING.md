# Phaseform Security Solutions — Brand Assets

This directory holds the Phaseform Security Solutions visual system as used by the Celestial runtime and the broader GoodShyt Group sovereign-AI stack. The brand expresses a single promise in two registers: *Safeguard your Domain, Secure your Dynasty*. Choose the register that fits the audience rather than mixing them in the same surface.

## The two treatments

The **Guardians of the Realm** treatment is the consumer- and story-facing register. It uses the gold-on-obsidian palette, the dragon-and-shield crest lineup, and the mountain-fortress backdrop. It belongs on hero imagery, pitch decks aimed at founders and operators, merchandise, physical handoff material, and anywhere the goal is to evoke authority, endurance, and dynastic protection. The dominant feeling is heraldic: a house mark earned, not stamped.

The **Tech Blue** treatment is the enterprise- and procurement-facing register. It uses the cool-blue and circuit-lattice palette with the same hex mark rendered in silver-blue. It belongs on security datasheets, compliance documentation, RFP responses, and any surface where the reader expects the visual grammar of enterprise infrastructure. The dominant feeling is operational: a control plane you can trust, legible at a glance.

Both treatments point at the same product. Do not mix them on the same page, slide, or document. Pick the audience, pick the register, stay in it.

## Tagline discipline

The brand carries a small number of taglines, each with a specific use:

*Safeguard your Domain · Secure your Dynasty* — the master tagline. Use on covers, masters, and any standalone brand surface.

*Fortify your Empire's Defenses — Protect. Detect. Respond.* — the capability tagline for collateral that has room to explain what the product does. Three-verb cadence is load-bearing; do not shorten.

*Advanced Industrial Cybersecurity* — the category descriptor. Use as a subtitle under the wordmark on enterprise surfaces.

*Guardians of the Realm* — the product-line name for the Celestial-integrated security suite. Capitalize both words.

## Color tokens

When implementing these treatments in code, use the canonical palette:

```
Obsidian   #090D14    # base, backdrop
Forest     #1C7552    # accent — stability, growth
Copper     #C46C2D    # accent — warmth, warning, earned trust
Cream      #E4DDD3    # text on dark
Slate      #51606D    # secondary text, dividers
```

The Guardians of the Realm treatment leans on Obsidian and Copper with gold leaf highlights; the Tech Blue treatment replaces Copper with a cool-blue accent while keeping Obsidian as the base.

## Don'ts

Do not recolor the crest lineup outside the two canonical treatments. Do not stretch, skew, or place the hex or shield marks against low-contrast backgrounds. Do not crop the dragon motifs out of the Guardians lockup — the heraldic composition reads as a unit. Do not render the wordmark without the "Security Solutions" descriptor on surfaces where the brand is introduced for the first time.
